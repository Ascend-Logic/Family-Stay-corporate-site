02About Nowhere Group

What we do and our name

WHAT WE DO

From the entrance to the exit of a lodging business, as one group.

From real estate utilization and transaction support to accommodation development, opening preparation, operations and cleaning, lodging sales and marketing, property management, and support for sale and resale. With accommodation development and operations at our core, we connect the specialist functions each project needs within the group.

Our strength is that we feed the issues and insights gained from cleaning and hotel operations into the development of the next property. Through this cycle, we aim to balance profitability with guest satisfaction.

OUR NAME

Creating something one of a kind, found nowhere else.

The name "Nowhere" carries our determination to create value and experiences that do not yet exist anywhere.

We discover the appeal of land and buildings, people and culture, and turn it into a stay that could only happen in that place. Creating a reason to say "I want to stay here" is where our work on every property begins.

05History

Our history

PHASE 01Founding and growth (2019–2024)

  1. 2019

    Established Nowhere LLC
    Launched the CLEANX9 cleaning service for lodging facilities

  2. 2021

    Turned around and achieved a buyout of the full-service hotel "Relaxia Izu Kogen"
    Total facilities produced exceeded 50
    Changed the company name to Nowhere Group Co., Ltd.

  3. 2023

    Total facilities produced exceeded 70

  4. 2024

    Total cleanings of lodging facilities exceeded 30,000

PHASE 02Second founding (2025–)

  1. 2025

    Made CLEANX9 Co., Ltd. a group company
    Total facilities produced exceeded 100
    Joined Airbnb Partners

  2. 2026

    Established VESTPORT Co., Ltd. and Family Stay Co., Ltd. as group companies
    Joined JTB's "Chiiki Mirai Nigiwai Kobo"
    Our hotel brand OMATSURI BASE won the Grand Prix at "BEST OF MINPAKU 2026"
    Sold the lodging real estate platform business
    Total cleanings of lodging facilities exceeded 80,000

06CEO Message

Message from the CEO

Nowhere Group Co., Ltd. - CEOGo Takeuchi

From the memories of travel, a new view in life.

An encounter or experience on a trip stays in your memory and becomes a place you want to return to someday. We want to create that "another sky" for each person, all across Japan.

Nowhere Group learned the business on the ground, in cleaning and hotel operations, and has carried the knowledge gained there into accommodation development.

What matters is not simply building beautiful facilities. It is creating a reason to visit a place, raising the satisfaction of every stay, and growing properties that keep being chosen. We find new possibilities in real estate and turn the appeal of each region into a lodging experience.

New value for travelers, for communities, and for everyone who works in lodging. One property at a time, we will build the future of Japan's lodging industry.

After working at a major financial institution, he launched and managed several businesses in lodging, real estate, food service and staffing. He founded Nowhere Group in 2019 and has since been involved in developing more than 100 lodging facilities. He connects knowledge from cleaning and hotel operations to accommodation development, and leads the group's collaborative lodging business.
