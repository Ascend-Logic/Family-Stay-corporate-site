From entrance
to exit.

Nowhere Group holds every function a lodging business needs within the group: real estate acquisition and distribution, planning and development, opening, operation, cleaning, sales and marketing, and finally sale and resale.

Rather than separating these functions, we feed what we learn on site back into the next plan and development, continuously raising profitability, operational efficiency and guest satisfaction.

01Philosophy

Group mission, vision and creed

Mission

Creating another sky in people's hearts, starting with travel.

Travel brings scenery, people, cultures and moments we never meet in everyday life. Starting from accommodation, we create new experiences and encounters, and another cherished place in people's hearts.

Vision

By 2030, become the lodging group chosen for travel satisfaction.

Building facilities is not all we do. Travelers, owners, local residents and the people who support lodging: we build a lodging business that creates value for each of them, and enrich Japan's lodging industry.

02CEO Message

Message from the CEO

Nowhere Group Inc. - CEOTsuyoshi Takeuchi

Building the future of lodging with insight accumulated in the field.

We learned the lodging business from the field, in cleaning and operation. We bring the issues that arise on site back into planning, and connect real estate, facility building, operation, cleaning and sales. Starting from accommodation, we keep creating new value for travelers, regions and operators.

I have always loved to travel, and visited some 30 countries in Europe and Asia as a student. Meeting people and cultures in so many places, I wanted to create hotels where guests can experience what is unique to each region. Drawing on my background in finance and on experience running restaurants and vacation rentals, I founded Nowhere LLC (now Nowhere Group Inc.) in 2019. Starting from the CLEANX9 cleaning service, we have expanded into operation, production, real estate, supply and resale, and grown into a group that covers the whole lodging value chain.

After working at a major financial institution, he launched and managed several businesses in lodging, real estate, food and staffing. He founded Nowhere Group Inc. in 2019 and has since been involved in producing more than 100 lodging facilities. He now leads the building of a lodging value chain covering real estate, operation, cleaning and resale.
