Creating and growing
the value of every stay.

Nowhere Group Inc. provides three services: hotel development & production, hotel operation and hotel supply. Working with our group companies and specialist partners, we combine the support each project needs, from creating the facility to opening, operation and continuous improvement.

01Services

Services of Nowhere Group Inc.

Hotel development & production

Produce

Turning a reason to stay into a viable lodging business.
Based on the market, location, building characteristics and business viability, we design the concept, space and guest experience, and operating model as one, and carry the project through to opening.

Read More

Hotel operation

Operation

Keeping a property the place guests choose, after opening.
For staffed and unstaffed properties. Through listing and sales management on major OTAs, pricing and inventory, reservations and guest service, and cleaning coordination, we work to improve quality and revenue.

Read More

Hotel supply

Supply

Everything a property needs, from opening to daily operation.
We support procurement of furniture, appliances, bedding, equipment, amenities and consumables, from pre-opening setup to continuous replenishment during operation.

03Strength

Improvement that starts in the field

From issues found on site
to the next property.

Our strength is that the issues and insights we gain from cleaning and hotel operation feed directly into the development and production of the next property.

01

Cleaning flow

Cleaning time, where supplies are kept and the spots that get dirty easily inform floor plans and equipment choices.

02

Guest feedback

Frequent confusion and requests in reviews and inquiries shape signage, equipment and the guest experience.

03

Operating costs

How often consumables are restocked and how often on-site support is needed guide specifications and operating models that are easy to run.

04

Issues found on site

Equipment trouble and flow problems found after opening are solved in advance in the next property.
