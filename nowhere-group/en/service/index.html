From planning and operation of lodging facilities
to supplying what they need.

Nowhere Group Inc. is the core company of the group, handling planning and production, opening support, operation and hotel supply. Real estate is handled by VESTPORT, cleaning and on-site operations by CLEANX9, and sales and marketing by Family Stay.

01Lodging Division

Lodging production and facility operation

Lodging Production

Turning a viable lodging business into reality, from the plan up.

We analyze the market, competitors, location and property, and design the target, financial plan, concept, space and guest experience. Beyond planning, we bring insight from real operation and cleaning sites into every facility.

  • Market and area research
  • Feasibility
  • Financial plan
  • Concept and brand
  • Space and design direction
  • Operation design
  • Opening support
  • Permits
  • Furniture and fixtures
  • Setup
  • Post-opening improvement
Facility Operation

Growing the value of a facility after it opens.

For hotels, vacation rentals and whole-house stays, we support day-to-day operation: sales, reservations, pricing, guest support and on-site operations, and feed data from sales, reviews and cleaning into continuous improvement.

  • OTA and reservations
  • Revenue management
  • Pricing
  • Inventory
  • Guest support
  • Check-in / check-out
  • Operations
  • Cleaning coordination
  • Review management
  • Occupancy improvement
  • Owner reporting

02Supply Division

Hotel supply service

Hotel Supply Service

Everything a lodging facility needs, delivered together.

Using the know-how and procurement network built through developing and operating lodging facilities, we supply furniture, appliances, bedding, fixtures, tableware, amenities, consumables and cleaning supplies, from bulk procurement at opening to ongoing replenishment.
