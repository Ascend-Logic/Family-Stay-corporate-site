Merit

Why choose us

Extensive experience

We have planned, designed, and operated a total of 72 facilities, and welcomed 20,000 groups. Facilities include private villas, resort hotels, urban business hotels, service apartment hotels, and other types of lodging.

One-stop service

Specialists in various fields, including first-class architects, administrative scriveners, revenue managers, financial planners, and photographers, work together to provide one-stop support for opening lodging facilities.

Outsourcing

We provide various services other than management, such as interior coordination, photography, on-site setup, permit application, room cleaning, etc., for both business to business as well as business to consumer.

Solution

What we offer

01

Operational planning and accommodation construction

We provide the marketing planning necessary for opening a business, including planning, concept design, and income/expense planning, as well as the detailed initial operational construction necessary for operation, including initial OTA construction, creation of user guides, and PA work.

02

Interior coordination

First-class architects and designers specializing in lodging facilities are set together with hotel producers to provide market-in interior coordination that reflects actual customer feedback.

03

Application for permit approvals

We can apply on your behalf for Ryokan business and Residential Accommodation business. We handle all three jurisdictions required to obtain permits: health department, fire department, and construction.

04

Photography

A professional photographer will take photos of your property for listing. We will direct the photography based on our operational experience and know-how, and take photos that will attract more customers.

05

Operation

We provide management services for a variety of lodging facilities, including resort hotels, service apartment hotels, and single-building rental hotels, offering attractive and differentiated hotel operations such as ICT-enabled unmanned and labor-saving hotels and food and activity plans in collaboration with local businesses.

06

Cleaning service

From whole house cleaning, garbage disposal, and replenishment of supplies to bed making, we provide cleaning services for a variety of lodging facilities, including hotels and private accommodations. We utilize manuals and information technology for each facility to improve quality and consistency.

07

Consulting

For those who are considering opening their own lodging facilities, our professionals, including designers, architects, and revenue managers, will provide advice and support.
