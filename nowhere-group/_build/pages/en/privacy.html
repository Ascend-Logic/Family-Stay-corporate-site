5. Outsourcing of personal information handling operations

[ When the Company outsources ]

We may outsource all or part of the operations handling personal information. In such cases, the Company will exercise necessary and appropriate supervision over the relevant subcontractor.

[ When the Company is entrusted ]

When we are entrusted with all or part of the personal information handling operations by companies, schools, organizations, etc. participating in our services or by our group companies, we handle the entrusted personal information in order to carry out the entrusted operations.

In order to improve or develop new services and conduct related research, analysis, and marketing, we may be entrusted by companies, schools, organizations, etc. participating in our services, and may add the user’s personal information held by us to the user’s personal information received from the company, school, or organization in question.

When we prepare and provide the results of analysis or distribution of advertisements as a commissioned service, we will provide such information after processing it into information that cannot be used to identify a specific individual by the said company, school, or organization alone, such as information on gender and age by excluding name and address information.

Notwithstanding the provisions of the preceding paragraph, in the following cases, the party to which the information is provided shall not fall under the category of a third party.

11. About cookies

The file sent as a cookie by our website does not contain any personally identifiable information. You can disable cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser.
