App

The Family Stay app

AI concierge “Haru”

From planning to booking,
family trips that move by conversation.

At the heart of Family Stay is Haru, a travel companion for families. Haru takes in your children’s ages and needs, the weather and travel time, and suggests places that suit your family right in the conversation. From finding a stay to booking and getting ready to leave, you can ask Haru from any screen.

  • Children’s ages, allergies, co-sleeping and other needs are applied automatically
  • Short vertical videos show the feel of a place before you stay
  • After booking, Haru gets ahead with packing lists and weather updates
01 — HARU

Just talk,
and find a stay that fits your family.

“Somewhere fun even in the rain over the October long weekend. Our youngest is one.” Send Haru a message like that, and it suggests stays that match, such as indoor pools or all-weather glamping, right in the conversation. Search and filters are there too when you want to set conditions in detail.

02 — SHORT PV

Feel the atmosphere
before you stay.

Each stay is introduced with short vertical videos, so you can check the rooms, activities and meals more vividly than in photos. The home screen shows what families with children of the same age are watching now, leading to unexpected destinations.

03 — FAMILY PROFILE

Tell us about your children
just once.

Children’s ages, co-sleeping or baby cots, baby food, allergies. Register them once in your family profile, and they are applied to your searches and passed on when you book. On each stay’s page, Haru adds the reasons it suits your family, so choosing takes less time.

04 — AFTER BOOKING

After booking,
Haru stays with your trip.

Reminders before the free cancellation deadline, a packing list for the children, weather and road conditions before you leave. Even after booking, Haru gets ahead of things to support your family’s trip. Allergies and other details are shared with the stay, so you can arrive with peace of mind.

* The screens shown are designs in development and may differ from the actual service. The app is in Japanese.
