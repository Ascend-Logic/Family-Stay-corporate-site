Working with our group companies and specialist partners, we combine the support each project needs, from creating the facility to opening, operation and continuous improvement.

01

Hotel development & production

Turning a reason to stay into a viable lodging business.

Based on the market, location, building characteristics and business viability, we design the concept, space and guest experience, and operating model as one, and carry the project through to opening. Issues and insights from cleaning and hotel operation feed into the design. Statutory design and construction are carried out with qualified external specialists.

Market and area research / Competitor research / Feasibility / Business plan / Target design / Concept development / Brand development / Space and guest experience design / Operations design / Opening preparation and setup / Licensing support / Post-opening improvement
