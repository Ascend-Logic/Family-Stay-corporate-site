What we do

01

Lodging production

Turning a viable lodging business into reality, from the plan.

We analyze the market, competitors, location and property, then design the target, business plan, concept, space and guest experience. We don't stop at planning: by feeding knowledge from our operation and cleaning teams into facility design, we build properties that balance guest satisfaction, profitability and operational efficiency.

Market and area research / Competitor research / Feasibility / Business plan / Target design / Concept development / Brand development / Space and design direction / Operations design / Opening support / Licensing support / Furniture and equipment selection / Setup / Post-opening improvement

02

Hotel operation

Growing the value of a property after opening.

For hotels, vacation rentals and whole-house rentals, we support daily operation from sales, reservation management and pricing to guest service and on-site operations. We analyze revenue, reviews, guest service and cleaning data to drive continuous improvement.

OTA and reservation management / Revenue management / Pricing / Inventory / Guest service / Check-in and check-out / Operations design / Cleaning coordination / Review management / Revenue and occupancy improvement / Owner relations / Reporting
