02About Nowhere Group

Who we are

From entrance to exit of a lodging facility,
as one group.

Nowhere Group holds every function a lodging business needs within the group: acquisition and distribution of real estate, planning and development, opening, operation, cleaning, sales and marketing, and finally sale and resale.

Rather than separating these functions, we feed what we learn on site back into the next project, continuously raising the profitability, operational efficiency and guest satisfaction of each property.

Nowhere Group
Creating what exists nowhere else.

The name "Nowhere" carries our commitment to create value and experiences that do not yet exist anywhere.

06CEO Message

Message from the CEO

Nowhere Group Inc. - CEOGo Takeuchi

Building the future of the lodging industry on knowledge earned in the field.

We learned the lodging business from the ground up, through cleaning and operation.

We bring the issues that arise on site back into planning, and connect real estate, facility development, operation, cleaning and sales.

Starting from accommodation, we keep creating new value for travelers, regions and operators.

After working at a major financial institution, he launched and managed several businesses in lodging, real estate, food service and staffing. He founded Nowhere Group Inc. in 2019 and has since produced more than 100 lodging facilities. Having expanded from on-site cleaning and operation into production, real estate, supply and resale, he now leads the building of a lodging value chain that covers everything from real estate to operation, cleaning and resale.
